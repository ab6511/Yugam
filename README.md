## 🛠️ Key Features Included

1. **Panknox-Inspired Blueprint Design & Telemetry**:
   - Dark obsidian industrial aesthetic (`#08090D`) with cyan and emerald accents.
   - Live telemetry status badges, ping rates, and blueprint grid overlays.
   - Zero-bureaucracy anti-agency positioning.

2. **The Market Gap (Interactive Comparison)**:
   - Contrasting the "Legacy Agency Default" vs the "YUGAM Growth Engine" across 5 critical dimensions: Operating Structure, Creative Quality, Attribution, Deployment Velocity, and Commercial Alignment.

3. **Viral Content Blueprint Matrix**:
   - Deconstructs proven high-converting short-form formats:
     - *The Industry Myth Sledgehammer* (Contrarian Wedge)
     - *The $10M System Teardown* (Curiosity & Architectural Reveal)
     - *The Real-Time Profit Audit* (Radical Transparency & Proof)
   - Scene-by-scene timing breakdowns, psychological trigger tags, and copyable script hooks.

4. **Interactive Profit & ROI Simulator**:
   - Real-time calculator letting prospective clients adjust their monthly revenue, ad spend, and average deal size.
   - Toggles between Conservative (2.4x) and Aggressive (3.8x) multipliers.
   - Calculates projected monthly run-rate, net profit lift, and CAC reduction amount.

5. **Verified Case Studies & Telemetry**:
   - Deep-dive proof from high-growth partners (DTC Health, B2B SaaS, Sustainable Luxury Apparel).
   - Before-and-after metrics, verified founder testimonials, and attribution tags.

6. **21-Day Sprint Deployment Protocol**:
   - Clear 4-phase timeline: Days 1-3 Diagnostic, Days 4-8 Script Engineering, Days 9-14 Studio Production, Days 15-21 Live Scaling.

7. **Interactive 4-Step Growth Diagnostic & Booking Flow**:
   - Dynamic qualifying wizard capturing company details, revenue tier, core bottleneck, and automated 30-min strategy session time-slot booking.

8. **The Dispatch // Live Operational Signal Terminal**:
   - Real-time terminal feed for algorithm updates, platform arbitrage insights, and newsletter dispatch capture.
